Technology ▾

Our holographic display builds on the same principles as projector technology. But instead of projecting onto a screen or wall, we project onto microscopic particles suspended in mid-air. These particles are held in place using "optical tweezers" and other optical techniques. Using light to suspend them, draw them into real 3D and 2D shapes, and illuminate them to reveal objects and scenes.
